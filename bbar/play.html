Rules cheat sheet

Setup

  • Each player owns the same 12 animals: 4 in hand, 8 in a face-down deck (nobody knows any deck order).
  • The opponent's hand is hidden; which of its animals it has not played yet is public.

A turn

  1. Play a card from your hand to the back of the queue.
  2. Act: the played animal does its action.
  3. Recurring: every hippo, crocodile and giraffe in the queue acts, front to back, each at most once per turn (a giraffe played this turn does not act again; a hippo or crocodile played this turn does).
  4. Five animals: if the queue holds exactly 5, the front 2 enter the bar (Heaven's Gate) and the last one is bounced (THAT'S IT).
  5. Draw the top card of your deck, if any.

Chameleon rulings

  • Copies any animal in the queue except a chameleon, using that animal's strength for the action only.
  • As a lion it is bounced (a lion is already there); as a monkey it triggers the monkey action; as a skunk it is not bounced; as a snake it sorts as strength 9; as a parrot or kangaroo it takes that animal's choice.

End and scoring (house rule)

  • The game ends as soon as fewer than 5 cards remain in hands, decks and queue combined. Animals still in the queue do not score.
  • Score = points of your animals in the bar. Higher score wins; equal scores draw.
  • The starting player alternates between games.

Keys

  • 1-4 pick a card of your hand; Tab or ← → move between cards or targets, Enter chooses; Enter plays the previewed move, Esc cancels.
